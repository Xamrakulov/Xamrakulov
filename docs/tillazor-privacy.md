# Privacy Policy for Tillazor (app.tillazor.uz)

**Last Updated: July 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and process your personal data when you use our application (the "Service"), which integrates with Meta Platforms, Inc. (including Instagram and Facebook APIs).

### 1. Information We Collect
To provide the Dashboard and messaging functionality, we access and collect the following data via Meta APIs (with your explicit consent):
* **Profile Information:** Instagram Username, Account ID, and Profile Picture.
* **Message Data:** Content of incoming and outgoing direct messages, media attachments (images, video, voice messages), and sender's username.

### 2. How We Use the Information
We process your data solely for the following purposes:
* To display messages in your personal customer support Dashboard.
* To enable you to reply to Instagram Direct Messages directly from the Dashboard.
* We **do not** sell, rent, or share this data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

### 3. Data Storage and Security
All message data is processed securely and stored only for the period necessary to synchronize your Dashboard or as required for your business operations. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access.

### 4. Third-Party Services
Our Service interacts with Meta APIs. By using our Service, you also agree to Meta's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

### 5. Data Deletion Instructions
You can request the deletion of your account and all associated Meta/Instagram data from our systems at any time. 
* To request data deletion, please contact us at: **info@innomart.uz**
* Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete all stored messages, tokens, and profile data associated with your account within 30 business days.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **info@innomart.uz**
